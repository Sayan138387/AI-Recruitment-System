### Step 1 — Understand the Problem Statement

Our project is called: AI-Based Recruitment and Resume Screening System

Let's break this title into parts.

AI-Based - 

This means our application will make intelligent decisions.
Instead of manually checking every resume, the computer will analyze resumes automatically.

Recruitment

Recruitment means hiring employees.

Imagine Google posts a Software Engineer job.

Within one week they receive:

5,000 resumes
10,000 resumes
Sometimes even 50,000 resumes

Can HR read every resume manually?

❌ Impossible.

That's why companies use ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems).
